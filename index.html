Forensic Glossary (English / Español)

Angle of Impact (Ángulo de impacto): The acute angle at which a droplet strikes a surface ($\arcsin(W/L)$).
Area of Convergence (Área de convergencia): The 2D location on a floor or wall where stain direction lines intersect.
Point of Origin (Punto de origen): The 3D $(X, Y, Z)$ point in space where the spatter event occurred.
Satellite Spatter (Salpicaduras satélite): Tiny secondary droplets formed around a main stain on rough surfaces or high impacts.
Confirmation Bias (Sesgo de confirmación): Focusing only on evidence that supports a pre-existing belief while ignoring contradictory data.
